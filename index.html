About

I finished my PhD in Electrical & Computer Engineering at Arizona State University in 2024. During it I interned at Stanford Research Institute and spent two summers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab, where most of my papers came from.

Since then I've been at Axio, building AI for education, joining as founding engineer and becoming Chief Product Officer. I'm looking for the next hard problem — at a frontier lab or a research org.

I'm also always open to collaborating. If you have an interesting idea, a research question, or just want to talk through a problem, send me a note. I read everything.
