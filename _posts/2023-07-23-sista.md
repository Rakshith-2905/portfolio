*ICML 2023 · ICASSP 2023 · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.13284.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/Rakshith-2905/SiSTA) · [Project page](https://rakshith-2905.github.io/SiSTA/) · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oHNaM_qXQeY)*

Deep networks generalize poorly when the target domain shifts away from the source. When target data is scarce, existing methods fall back on synthetic augmentations, but those struggle with large distribution shifts.

**SiSTA** (Single-Shot Target Augmentations) fine-tunes a generative model using a *single* target sample, then uses new sampling strategies to generate synthetic target data. It outperforms existing methods on binary and multi-class problems, handles a range of distribution shifts, and gets close to models trained on the full target dataset.

## Method

![SiSTA pipeline]({{ '/assets/img/posts/sista/pipeline.png' | relative_url }})

1. **Source training:** train the source classifier and a StyleGAN-v2 generator on source data.
2. **Single-shot StyleGAN fine-tuning:** fine-tune the source generator on one target example (SiSTA-U).
3. **Synthetic data generation:** sample the target generator's latent space to build a synthetic target dataset (SiSTA-G).
4. **Source-free UDA:** adapt the source classifier on that synthetic data.

![Examples of synthetic data generated with SiSTA]({{ '/assets/img/posts/sista/website-fig-teaser.png' | relative_url }})

More examples across benchmarks and shifts: [icml-sista.github.io](https://icml-sista.github.io/).

## Results

On face-attribute detection, SiSTA improves 1-shot source-free adaptation across increasing shift severity and a suite of image corruptions, and combined with standard augmentations it matches full-target domain adaptation.
