*ICASSP 2024 · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.04838.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/LLNL/CREPE)*

Can vision-language models like CLIP predict how objects in an image relate to each other, and simplify the complex graphical models that usually combine visual and language cues for this?

**CREPE** (CLIP Representation Enhanced Predicate Estimation) uses the UVTransE framework to build translational embeddings for the subject, object and union boxes in a scene, using CLIP's language capabilities. A new contrastive training method refines the union-box prompts. On Visual Genome, CREPE improves on the previous state of the art by **15.3%**, with mR@20 of 31.95.
